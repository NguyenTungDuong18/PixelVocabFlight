WORDWING 0.13 — PIXEL 1944 (2026-11-04)

All active artwork (player fighter, four bomber liveries, aircraft carrier,
rockets, supplies, explosions and effects, interface frames, icons, logo,
battlefield cards and the title-screen airbase) is original pixel art drawn
procedurally by Tools/PixelForge in this project, one PNG per animation frame:
Assets/_Project/Art/Resources/Pixel. No external images are used.
Fictional setting inspired by 1940s aviation; no real insignia or factions.

Terrain: Scripts/Art/PixelTerrain.cs (project code).
Typeface: VT323 by The VT323 Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1
(see VT323-OFL.txt).
Earlier artwork (0.10–0.12) is retired from the build; originals remain in
SourceAssets/PixelArt. Vocabulary and speech attribution are unchanged.
