## Exact prompt

Create one new EXTREMELY WIDE panoramic top-down pixel-art ocean water background, aspect ratio 3:1, ideally 3072 by 1024 pixels, for a scrolling arcade airplane game. Use the three supplied images ONLY as style, palette and wave-pattern references. Extend that same deep turquoise-blue sea across the whole wide canvas. This is a small-scale, zoomed-out water tile: many delicate small pixel wavelets, low contrast cyan foam accents, a few tiny distant fish shadows. The waves and any underwater details should appear noticeably smaller than in the supplied portrait images. Keep the entire image predominantly uninterrupted open water; no large rocks, islands, buoys, floating crates, boats, aircraft, horizon or text. No borders or framing. Make opposite edges visually seamless for repeated scrolling, with consistent blue colour at every edge. Orthographic straight-down view, calm lively ocean, crisp square pixel clusters and subtle water variations, matching the reference images' painterly pixel-art style. This background will fill extra space around the user's smaller original ocean images, so colour consistency and understated scale are essential.
