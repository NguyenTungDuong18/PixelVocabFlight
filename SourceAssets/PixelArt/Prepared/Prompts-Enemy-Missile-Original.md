## Enemy prompt

Create a brand-new original pixel-art enemy airplane sprite for a fictional top-down arcade learning game. A compact angular green jet with a long central fuselage, stepped swept wings, two short tail fins, pale mint cockpit, dark pixel outlines, olive and forest-green panels, and two tiny orange engine glows. Design a distinct original silhouette. Top-down orthographic view, nose straight UP, symmetric, clear and readable at 64 pixels. One single aircraft only, centered, fills 85 percent of a square canvas. Genuine transparent alpha background. Crisp intentionally chunky square pixels, flat limited palette, no smoothing, no floor, no shadow, no scene, no text, no logos, no borders.

## Missile prompt

A small stylized olive-green rocket with a rounded cream nose, a single copper-orange band, four simple stepped dark-green fins, black pixel outlines and a tiny orange engine flame. Nose points diagonally to the UPPER RIGHT, tail to lower left. One single isolated object centered and filling 85 percent of a square canvas. Distinct original design, simple chunky square pixels with a limited palette, easy to recognize at 48 pixels. Genuine transparent alpha background. No scene, no floor, no shadow, no text, no logo, no borders.
